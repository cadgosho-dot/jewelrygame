# JEWELRYxJEWELRY v0.10.553 Implementation Notes

## Change
- Changed the player artisan-level requirement for hiring workshop staff from **Lv.15 or higher** to **Lv.10 or higher**.
- Updated both the actual unlock判定 and the condition text shown on the workshop staff screen.

## Unchanged conditions
- Player-crafted cumulative total: 100
- Workshop level: 15 or higher
- Required workshop tools and equipment
- Store 1 expansion
- Store 2 contract
- Store 1 and Store 2 both Lv.5 or higher
- Cumulative store sales: 300
- Delivered orders: 50
- No outstanding rent, workshop fees, or wages

## Compatibility
- Existing save data is preserved.
- Players who already hired workshop staff are unaffected.
- Players at artisan Lv.10–14 can unlock hiring immediately after satisfying every other condition.
